## Fee policy

The creation-time trading fee is the fixed Rehype hook rate from 1% through 3% in 0.25% steps, default 1%. Rehype receives equal `startFee`/`endFee` of `tradingFeeBps × 100` ppm and `durationSeconds=0`; DEAD control preserves the fixed schedule after creation. The LP fee remains 0.05%, while PoolKey retains the dynamic-fee marker `0x800000`. Token-specific fee displays label the nominal hook-plus-LP total, such as 3% + 0.05% = 3.05%; general policy pages do not assert one total for every pool. Executable quotes include actual contract costs, including the existing Bundler first-buy exemption described above.
